# 18th-Century Dutch Maritime Textile Trade Analysis (1700–1724)

## 📌 Research Question

> **"How did Dutch maritime trade networks (VOC vs. WIC) distribute high-value Asian and European textile commodities across global port nodes, and what macroeconomic trends governed trade volume and revenue in Guldens between 1700 and 1724?"**

This project investigates the trade dynamics, revenue fluctuations, and geographic distribution of the 18th-century Dutch maritime textile trade using historical transaction data from **VOC (Vereenigde Oostindische Compagnie)** and **WIC (West-Indische Compagnie)** shipment logs.

---

## 💡 Key Analytical Findings

1. **Company Dominance (VOC vs. WIC):** The **VOC** overwhelmingly dominated maritime textile trade value, generating **90.75M Guldens (97.08%)** of total recorded trade value (~93.48M Guldens total), compared to **2.73M Guldens (2.92%)** generated by the **WIC**.
2. **Peak Trade Expansion (1720–1723):** Trade revenue experienced a major spike in the early 1720s, reaching an all-time peak of **14.5M Guldens in 1723**, following a sharp drop and near-stagnation between 1716 and 1720.
3. **Primary Geographic Hubs:** **Jakarta (former Batavia)** served as the paramount trade origin hub by total value in Guldens, followed by **Amsterdam**, **Zeeland (Middelburg)**, and **Nagasaki (Deshima)**.
4. **Volume & Shipment Scale:** Across the 25-year period (1700–1724), the dataset registers **23K total shipments** encompassing **29.37M total units of quantity**.

### 1. Data Preprocessing & Staging (`rawdata_textiles.csv` → `staged_cleaned_textiles.csv`)

* Ingested historical maritime trade logs containing shipment details, product quantities, port identifiers, and total valuations in Dutch Guldens.
* Cleaned missing categories and standardized fields across foreign and domestic origins for downstream SQL processing.

### 2. SQL Server (Data Engineering & Transformation)

* Executed relational schema joins between product dimension tables (`dim_textiles`), port dimension tables (`dim_ports`), and the core transaction table (`fact_textile_trade`).
* **Data Quality Maintenance:** Identified unmapped or blank textile categories (`WHERE category IS NULL OR category = ''`) and updated records to ensure clean reporting:
  ```sql
  UPDATE dbo.dim_textiles
  SET category = 'Uncategorized'
  WHERE category IS NULL OR TRIM(category) = '';
  ```
* **Analytical Queries:**
  * **Temporal Trends:** Calculated annual totals for trade value (`total_value_guldens`) and volume (`quantity`) across exchange years (`1700–1724`).
  * **Company Performance:** Aggregated total volume and financial value grouped by trading entity (`company`: VOC vs. WIC).
  * **Port Rankings:** Evaluated top 5 origin ports by total trade value in Guldens using `TOP 5` and `GROUP BY` on joined port dimensions (`dim_ports`).

### 3. Power BI (Data Visualization & Dashboard Architecture)

Built an executive analytics dashboard (`Dutch Project.pdf`) featuring key KPI cards and multi-visual layouts:

* **Executive Summary KPIs:** Highlighted core metrics—**93.48M Total Guldens**, **29.37M Total Quantity**, and **23K Total Shipments**.
* **Timeline Analysis Line Chart:** Mapped `Total Guldens by year` from 1700 to 1724, identifying key historic peaks (1711 at 8.5M, 1723 at 14.5M) and drops (1716–1720).
* **Company Share Donut Chart:** Visualized the revenue split between **VOC (90.75M / 97.08%)** and **WIC (2.73M / 2.92%)**.
* **Geographic Distribution Bar Chart:** Visualized `Total Guldens by Ports`, showcasing top nodes led by **Jakarta (Batavia)**, **Amsterdam**, **Zeeland**, and **Nagasaki (Deshima)**.

## 📐 Metric Definitions

* **Total Guldens (`total_value_guldens`):** Real historical transaction value expressed in 18th-century Dutch Guldens (Florins).
* **Quantity (`quantity`):** Total count/units of textile goods moved per shipment.
* **Shipment Count (`shipment_id`):** Distinct count of completed maritime trade voyages logged across the trade network.
